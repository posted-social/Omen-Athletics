body

hot & cold

Heat slows everyone down. Cold wakes everyone up. We use both on purpose, and we use them together.

Hot therapy

Bible talks in the sauna.

Our sauna seats 20, not 4. Phones stay outside, nobody is going anywhere for twenty minutes, and the conversation gets honest fast. We read Scripture together, talk about it plainly, and let the heat do its work on body and mind at the same time.

"Come to me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest."Matthew 11:28

Cold therapy

The cold plunge.

Two to three minutes in cold water is a controlled dose of stress. You learn to breathe through the shock, to stay when everything says leave, and to notice how clear your head is afterward. We use it as a regular practice for mental health maintenance, not a stunt. Coaches walk you through your first plunge.
